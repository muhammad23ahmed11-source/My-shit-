How it works

From first visit to first PR

A simple, repeatable process. You always know what you're doing today and why.

  1. 1

    Assess

    We talk goals and history, then test movement, strength, and conditioning. No judgment, no max-out tests.

    Free · 45 min
  2. 2

    Plan

    You get a written 4-week training block with clear targets for every lift and a realistic weekly schedule.

    Within 48 hours
  3. 3

    Train

    Coached sessions where every set is logged. Weights go up when your form and recovery say they should.

    2–4× per week
  4. 4

    Retest

    Every four weeks we retest and compare numbers, then build the next block from what we learned.

    Every 4 weeks

FAQ

Common questions

I'm out of shape. Is this for me?

Yes. Most clients start out of practice. Your program starts where you are today and progresses from there.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable clothes, athletic shoes, and a water bottle. Tell me about any injuries or medical conditions beforehand.

Do you help with nutrition?

Yes. Habit-based coaching is included in online and 1-on-1 packages: protein targets, meal structure, and simple swaps. No crash diets.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule at least 12 hours ahead at no cost. Later cancellations count as a session.

Do you train teens?

Yes, ages 13 and up with a parent's consent. Youth sessions focus on technique, speed, and injury prevention for their sport.
