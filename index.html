While you wait

What to do in a plumbing emergency

  1. Shut off the water. Close the fixture valve, or the main shutoff (often near the front hose bib or in the garage).
  2. Turn off the water heater if the leak is near it. Switch gas to "pilot" or flip the breaker for electric.
  3. Move valuables and electronics away from water, and avoid outlets near standing water.
  4. Call us. We'll talk you through anything else while our plumber drives over.
